03 / KNOW THE LIMITS

Evidence with context.

Educational attainment describes adult residents, not the quality of local schools. Use these estimates to ask better questions—not to infer individual ability or explain why a disparity exists.

One measure. Consistent comparisons.

Only unstratified estimates with valid percentages are included. Each view holds geography, population group, and reporting period consistent. No overlapping areas are averaged together.

Historical data, transparently labeled.

The bundled snapshot covers 2000 through 2015. ACS five-year estimates summarize an entire period; they are not measurements for its final year. The 2000 Census and ACS also use different collection methods.
